TAIFEX Institutional Traders

Futures Analytics Showcase

Open Data • Taiwan Futures Exchange

Track what the big players are doing

This site showcases daily institutional trading and open interest data for Taiwan futures contracts. Data comes directly from the TAIFEX open data portal covering Dealers (自營商), Investment Trusts (投信), and Foreign Institutional Investors (外資及陸資).

What the dashboard reveals

Net Positions
Long minus short volume and open interest by contract
Institution Comparison
Side-by-side view of Dealers vs Investment Trusts vs FINI
Trading & OI Trends
Volume and unsettled positions across major futures products
Source: TAIFEX MarketDataOfMajorInstitutionalTradersDetailsOfFuturesContractsBytheDate  •  Raw CSV downloaded daily, cleaned and stored in SQLite.
